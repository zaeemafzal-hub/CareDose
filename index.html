Email setup (EmailJS)

This app has no server of its own, so real email needs a connected email service. EmailJS has a free tier for sending email directly from a browser app. Create a service + template there, then paste the three IDs below. Your template can use variables {{to_email}}, {{med_name}}, {{dosage}}, {{dose_time}}.
